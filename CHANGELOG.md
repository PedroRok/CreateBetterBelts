# Create Better Belt 0.1.1 (beta) - Minecraft 1.21.1

- Fixed the Inline Belt casing being rotated on Fabric (the casing models relied on a NeoForge-only model transform)
- Inline Belt: a belt that runs through a shaft and is placed already encased (Andesite or Brass Casing, picked from the closest casing in your hotbar)
- Cross-axis belts with the Belt Connector
- Reverse any belt with the Wrench; the rotation direction indicator shows on every segment
- Fabric and NeoForge, requires Create 6.0+
